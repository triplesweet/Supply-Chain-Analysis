# Supply-Chain-Analysis

**Business Need?**
> A Supply Manager needs a multi use dashboard that can simplify performance for executive reporting and be used daily for inventory analysis. Interactive Tableau Dashboard can be found here.

> Note: This excercise is based on a generated data set. Any business insights found should not be applied to any external market or industry.

**About The Data**
> This data is compiled of 100+ product codes within the beauty and skincare industry. It records each products SKU code, Price, Revenue, Availability, Total Sales, Orders, Leads, and Target Demographic.

**Business Questions?**
> Which SKU codes are low in stock?

> Can we meet the order amount with/without pending stock?

> What categories are performing best?

> What demographics are the most profitable?

> How much will revenue will be made in the future based on current lead generation?

> Which product catalog should be expanded? What price is the best for this new SKU code?

> What products should be discontinued?

**Programs Used?**
> **_Data Cleaning_** - Microsoft - Excel

> **_SQL_** - PostgreSQL - Postico, BigQuery

> _**Data visualization, Dashboarding**_ - Salesforce - Tableau

**Insights and Recommendations**
> Cosmetics and Skincare are high risk in Inventory with many not being able to satisfy incoming orders with current or pending stock. This is especially concerning for skincare as they're outpacing the other categories in active orders and leads, indicating there is not likely to be a decline soon.
> > **recommendation:** Prioritize wrapping up production for skincare and comsetics and drop available stock in at risk products in category order skincare > cosmetics > haircare.

> Male haircare, women's cosmetics and womens skincare produce the most revenue across the catelog, with only women's skincare reaching over $70k. Women targeted proudct also lead in Sales and Active Orders; however their haircare is the lowest category in the catalog at less than 15K in revenue.
> > **recommendation:** Do an in depth analysis of the past years haircaire line and consider removing low profit lines. Alternatively, increasing lead generation in months highlighted by the YTY analysis may also bring better success.

**Next Steps**
> **Supply Manager:** Obtain yearly records of these performance metrics and send them to a data analyst.

> **Data analyst:** Use previous years of data t oestablish a predictive model for monthly revenue, category performance, leads, and stock levels.
> > **WHY?**
> > To prevent extreme stock levels risks in the future and improve executive presentation with Y2Y/ M2M comparisons that better simplifiy performance decreases or improvements.
