# Supply-Chain-Analysis
Business Need?
A Supply Manager needs a multi use dashboard that can simplify performance for executive reporting and be used daily for inventory analysis.

<img width="1710" height="935" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-11 at 5 26 16 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b464082f-3209-490e-a22a-8454c25f44b2" />

Business Questions?
> Which SKU codes are low in stock? Can we meet the order amount with/without pending stock?
> What categories are performing best? What demographics are the most profitable?
> How much will revenue will be made in the future based on current lead generation?
> Which product catalog should be expanded? What price is the best for this new SKU code?
> What products should be discontinued?

Programs Used?
Data Cleaning > Microsoft - Excel
SQL Query > PostgreSQL - Postico, BigQuery
Data visualization, Dashboarding > Salesforce - Tableau
